Challenge

Across needed to grow owned social and Discord audiences steadily over time, not just around one-off launches, while keeping community quality high enough to support DAO governance participation and long-term retention.

Approach

  • Built and ran a structured ambassador program to extend reach through trusted community voices instead of relying on paid growth
  • Recruited, trained, and led a dedicated mods team to keep Discord quality high as it scaled from 10K to 70K members
  • Gave the community team clear ownership of engagement, support triage, and sentiment reporting so growth didn't outpace moderation capacity
  • Coordinated ambassador and mod activity with DAO communications so community-led growth stayed aligned with governance and protocol updates

Result

Sustained, compounding growth across Late 2024–July 2026: social following grew from 60K to 125K and Discord grew from 10K to 70K members, without a single campaign carrying the growth — the ambassador and mod programs kept scaling reach and quality together throughout the tenure.
